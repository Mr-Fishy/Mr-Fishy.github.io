Welcome to my portfolio! Here is a selection of some of my recent work. Including small scoped algorithm design, as well as broader systems engineering.

## Algorithms & Analysis

### [Unit-Time Greedy Task Scheduling](https://github.com/Mr-Fishy/greedy-task-scheduling)

A small algorithm implementation project to design and implement a solution to the unit-time task scheduling problem with deadlines and penalties.

### [Divide & Conquer Polynomial Multiplication](https://github.com/Mr-Fishy/fft-polynomial-multiplication)

Another small implementation project to design and implement a solution to polynomial multiplication using a divide and conquer method. This project utilizes the Fast Fourier Transform to achieve the result.

### [Power Implementation Analysis](https://github.com/Mr-Fishy/power-implementation-tests)

A research project to implement, test, and analyze the difference between iterative and recursive power functions.

## System Projects

### [Falcon Racing 2025](https://github.com/uniqueident/Baja-2025)

As a lead contributor to the codebase, in 2025 I designed and led development in an embedded system to operate on a Baja SAE vehicle to track engine RPM, CVT temperature, and other electrical interfaces.

### [Ocean Engine](https://github.com/Lefihm/Ocean)

As a long-term side project, as eventually a sunsetted project. I lead and brought in peers to play with complex game engine design concepts.
